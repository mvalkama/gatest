Our story

Kahvila Kaarna opened in 2019 in a wooden house from the 1920s, a short walk from the river. The house had been a bakery once, and the old oven is still in the back room. We don’t bake in it, but we like having it there.

The people

Aino runs the café and the kitchen. Her brother Eero bakes: the first buns go into the oven at six, and the rye bread for lunch at seven. In summer two students from the local catering school join us on the terrace.

Where our food comes from

Flour from a mill in Kurikka, berries from farms around Seinäjoki, fish from the Kvarken coast on Tuesdays and Fridays. When something runs out, it’s gone until tomorrow.
